Detection and response

Day to day in the SOC: triage, investigation, containment and clear communication.

  • Led investigations into confirmed compromises

    AiTM phishing, credential theft, DCSync attempts, malware infections, persistence and post-exploitation. Correlated identity, endpoint, email, network and threat-intelligence evidence to establish scope, root cause and user impact.

  • Worked high-fidelity EDR alerts end to end

    3–4 per shift, including multi-stage malware taking about an hour each from analysis through remediation and updates to affected users and management.

  • Traced multi-stage attack chains

    Execution, persistence, privilege escalation, lateral movement and command-and-control across endpoint, identity and network telemetry, using Defender XDR, CrowdStrike Falcon, Sentinel and Chronicle SOAR.

  • Responded in OT production environments

    Monitored and contained malware on legacy Windows 7 and 10 systems, including spread through removable-media software transfers, while keeping production running where isolating an endpoint would have stopped it.

  • Hunted proactively

    PowerShell telemetry, IOC-driven searches, CrowdStrike endpoint data and Metabase analysis to surface suspicious execution and persistence.

Security engineering

Building the tools, logic and process that let the SOC investigate faster and with less noise.

Alert tuning across client environments

Led cross-functional tuning as new clients came online: refining detection conditions, suppression and severity until analysts saw signal instead of noise.

500–700+ alerts per client, per shift, at onboarding

  • Email-security triage framework

    A reusable investigation framework that brings everything about a suspicious email into one view: sender and authentication results, links, attachments, user clicks, remediation and user reports. It adds automated risk indicators and a score-based verdict, and covers reported emails, malicious URLs, URL clicks and AiTM phishing.

    5–15 min to 1 mininitial triage per alert, across 50–70+ email alerts per shift

  • Risky sign-in playbook and SOP

    The official KQL-based playbook for identity-risk investigations. Correlates interactive and non-interactive sign-ins, audit, Office activity, inbox rules and Entra ID risk to judge compromise and token persistence.

    15 analysts, 8 clientsadopted for 20+ investigations a day

  • Detection logic refinement

    Worked with engineering to cut false positives: for example, moving curl detections from broad command-line substring matching to process-aware execution conditions.

    Fewer false positivesplus suppression and severity calibration across identity, endpoint and email
